<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the tool">
    **Request access** lists everything your team can ask for. Search by name or level, or filter by category. Levels you already hold are ticked. Privileged levels are marked with a shield.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a level and a duration">
    Pick the access level and how long you need it. The choices stop at the resource's longest allowed duration. You can ask to extend later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say why">
    The reason goes to the approvers and stays on the record. It's required unless the resource is pre-approved, and always required for privileged levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it">
    The approvers are notified by email and, if [Slack](/integrations/slack) is connected, by direct message. The request page shows its progress and who it's waiting on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requesting for someone else

Admins can request for anyone, and managers for the people who report to them. Turn on **Anyone can request for someone else** in **Settings → General** to let everyone do it (useful for team leads setting up new hires). For a new starter's whole set of access at once, use [onboarding](/guides/onboarding-and-transfers).

## Extending and giving up access

On **Access → Mine**, time-limited grants have an **Extend** button that opens a new request for the same level, categorised as an *Extension*. **Give up** opens a removal for access you no longer need.

<Note>
  You can't have two open requests for the same level. If one is already waiting, the catalog links to it instead.
</Note>

## Comments and cancelling

Anyone involved in a request can comment on it. Comments notify the requester, the person it's for, and whoever it's waiting on. You can cancel your own request while it's awaiting approval.
