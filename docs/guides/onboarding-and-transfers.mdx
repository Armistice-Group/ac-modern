title: Onboarding and transfers
description: Request a new hire's starting access in one go, and change someone's access when they move roles.

Both work from a person's page (**People**, or **Your team** for managers) and are available to admins and to the person's manager. They create ordinary requests, grouped together, with one reason on every request. Each request still goes to its own approvers: that's the evidence auditors look for.

## Onboarding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the person">
    They need to be a member already: invited, or added by [directory sync](/identity/directory-sync).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Onboard">
    Write what they're joining as, for example *"New backend engineer, starts Monday"*. This becomes the justification on every request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add their access">
    Add resources and levels one by one, or **copy a teammate's access**: pick someone in the same role and their grants are added (minus anything the new hire already has).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    One request opens per item, categorised as **New hire**, and the approvers are notified as usual.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Transfers

**Transfer** does the same for someone changing roles, plus:

- **Remove**: tick the access they no longer need. Removals open straight away, no approval needed.
- **New title**, and for admins **New manager**. The new manager is set first, so they're the one who approves the new access.

Requests from a transfer are categorised as **Transfer**, and removals carry the reason *Transfer*.

<Note>
  You can't onboard or transfer yourself. Ask your manager or an admin.
</Note>
