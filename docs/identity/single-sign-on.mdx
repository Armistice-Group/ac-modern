ac·modern supports any OpenID Connect provider: Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, JumpCloud, Authentik, Keycloak and others. Each organisation configures its own in **Settings → Single sign-on**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register ac·modern with your IdP">
    Create an OIDC web application with this redirect URI:

    ```
    https://<your ac·modern>/auth/oidc/callback
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it">
    Enter the **issuer URL** (for example `https://acme.okta.com`), **client ID**, **client secret** and a **button label**. Scopes default to `openid email profile`. **Test connection** checks discovery.

    The issuer must use `https`. Self-hosted installs may use plain `http` on a LAN.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your email domains">
    Add the domains your people sign in with (`acme.com`). Sign-ins are only trusted for these domains.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Email domains

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cloud">
    A domain must be verified before it counts. Add the TXT record shown next to it:

    ```
    acme.com.  TXT  "ac-modern-verification=<token>"
    ```

    then click **Verify**. Only one organisation can claim a domain.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Self-hosted">
    Domains are trusted as soon as you add them.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How people are matched

1. Someone who has signed in before is matched by their IdP subject.
2. Otherwise by email: an existing member (invited, or from directory sync) is linked.
3. Otherwise, if their email is on a verified domain and **Create accounts on first sign-in** is on, they become a member.

People from other domains need an invite. Offboarded people stay out, even if your IdP still lets them in.

## Signing in

- **Self-hosted:** the sign-in page shows **Continue with &lt;label&gt;**.
- **Cloud:** **Sign in with SSO** asks for a work email and finds the organisation by domain.
- **From your IdP's app dashboard:** use `https://<your ac·modern>/auth/oidc/start?org=<org slug>`. The exact link is shown in settings.

## Requiring SSO

**Require single sign-on** makes members sign in through your IdP, so disabling someone there locks them out here too. A member with a password session is sent through SSO. It needs at least one verified domain.

<Note>
  **Owners keep password sign-in** as a break-glass, in case your IdP is down or misconfigured.
</Note>

## Configuring with environment variables

Self-hosted installs can set SSO in the environment instead (this overrides the settings page):

```sh
ACM_OIDC_ISSUER=https://acme.okta.com
ACM_OIDC_CLIENT_ID=…
ACM_OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET=…
ACM_OIDC_NAME=Okta
ACM_OIDC_SCOPES="openid email profile"
ACM_OIDC_DOMAINS=acme.com
ACM_OIDC_REQUIRED=false
```
