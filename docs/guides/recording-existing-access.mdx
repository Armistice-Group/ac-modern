When you start, people already have access. Record it so reviews and offboarding cover it:

<Frame>
  <img src="/screenshots/access.png" alt="Who has what" />
</Frame>

**Access → Record existing access** (admins): pick the resource and level, the people who have it, an optional end date, and **where it came from**, for example *"Granted before ac·modern (IT sheet)"*.

<Warning>
  Recorded access skips the request process, so the source note is required, and every recorded grant is listed under **Access outside requests** in the [audit evidence](/compliance/audit-evidence). Use it for your starting point, not as a shortcut afterwards.
</Warning>

If your apps are in Authentik, Okta or Entra ID, [import them from your IdP](/guides/importing-apps) instead: it creates the resources and records who has each one in one go.

If the access lives in IdP groups, [map those groups](/identity/directory-sync#group-mappings) instead and it's detected automatically.

## Exporting

**Access → Export CSV** downloads what's shown: person, email, resource, level, status, source, granted and expiry dates.
