<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the price">
    In Stripe, create a product with a **recurring, per-unit** price (for example $4 per month). Each unit is one active person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the environment">
    ```sh
    ACM_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_live_…
    ACM_STRIPE_PRICE_ID=price_…
    ACM_PRICE_LABEL="$4 per person / month"
    ACM_FREE_SEATS=5
    ACM_TRIAL_DAYS=14
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the webhook">
    Point a Stripe webhook at `https://<your ac·modern>/api/billing/webhook` with these events:

    - `checkout.session.completed`
    - `customer.subscription.created`, `.updated`, `.deleted`, `.paused`, `.resumed`

    Set its signing secret as `ACM_STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. Events are verified, and anything older than five minutes is rejected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Customer Portal">
    In Stripe's settings, enable the Customer Portal with payment method updates, invoice history and cancellation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What ac·modern does

- Creates a Stripe customer per organisation, tagged with its id
- Starts Checkout with a quantity equal to active members, with a trial on the first subscription only
- Updates the subscription quantity when the team changes (checked on the job interval, prorated)
- Treats `trialing`, `active` and `past_due` as paid. Everything else is the free tier.

<Note>
  Without `ACM_STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, billing is off and every organisation has everything.
</Note>
