Once approved, a request moves to **Being set up**. How it gets done depends on the resource's setup method.

<Frame>
  <img src="/screenshots/resource-editor.png" alt="Resource settings: levels, approval and setup" />
</Frame>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="By hand">
    The resource's fulfillers (else its owner, else admins) get a task: under **Requests → To set up**, on the overview, by email and in Slack. They make the change in the tool, then click **Mark done**, optionally with a note (*"Invited to the org"*). The grant starts then, and its expiry is counted from that moment.

    Removals work the same way: the task says what to take away, and **Mark done** ends the grant.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Integration">
    With a built-in integration, ac·modern makes the change itself and completes the request with a note of what it did, for example *"GitHub: @dev added to team platform as member"*.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Webhook">
    A signed POST to your own script or automation (n8n, Zapier, a Lambda). A 2xx answer completes the request. See [Webhooks](/integrations/webhooks).
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## When automation fails

If a webhook or integration fails (an error, a timeout, a person with no account in the IdP), the request stays **Being set up**. The error goes into its history, and the fulfillers get the task by hand with the reason. They can fix the cause and **Retry**, or do it themselves and **Mark done**.

<Tip>
  Approval always comes first. Nothing is set up until every approval step is done, and the evidence shows setup time after approval time.
</Tip>
