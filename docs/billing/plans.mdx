Billing only exists in cloud mode, and only when the operator has set up Stripe. Self-hosted installs have every feature, free.

## Seats

You pay for **active people**. Offboarded and deactivated people don't count. Seats follow your team automatically, prorated.

## Free tier

Up to a set number of people (5 unless the operator changed it) with no card. Over the limit without a plan:

- **Paused:** inviting people, creating accounts through SSO, and adding people from directory sync
- **Still working:** everything else, including requests, approvals, removals, offboarding, reviews and evidence

Admins see a banner and the reason. Approvals, removals and offboarding are never blocked over billing.

## Upgrading

**Settings → Billing → Upgrade to Team** opens Stripe Checkout, with a trial the first time. **Manage billing** opens the Stripe Customer Portal for cards, invoices and cancellation.

If a payment fails, the plan stays in place while Stripe retries, and admins see a banner asking them to update the card. A cancelled plan returns to the free tier: nothing is deleted.
